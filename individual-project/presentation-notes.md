# One-minute presentation

**Teresa Tu - STATS 401**

Keep the single slide on screen throughout.

> Information is Beautiful's bubble timeline invites readers to explore major data breaches across time. Its large circles make scale memorable, but dense labels and crowded events make smaller incidents difficult to find.
>
> My D3 redesign preserves proportional circle areas and the timeline. It separates circles within each year and lets readers focus on a period while retaining the full overview.
>
> A searchable named list reveals event stories. Readers can select up to three events and compare their reported quantities with bars sharing a zero baseline.
>
> Tiny circles still need focusing, and area alone remains difficult to compare. Forty uncertain or incompatible counts stay in the named list instead of receiving invented sizes.

Suggested timing: original purpose and critique, 0-15 seconds; timeline and focus, 15-35 seconds; event details and comparison, 35-50 seconds; limitations, 50-60 seconds.
