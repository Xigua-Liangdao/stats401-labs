# One-minute presentation

**Teresa Tu - STATS 401**

Keep the single slide on screen throughout.

> Information is Beautiful's bubble timeline makes major data breaches memorable, but dense circles and uneven labels make smaller events difficult to find.
>
> I preserve its overview with two views. A bubble timeline keeps area proportional to reported quantity. A coordinate view uses year and logarithmic size. It groups nearby events in the same year, labels the count, and shows their size range.
>
> Both keep a full timeline while readers focus on selected years. A named list opens event stories. Up to three selected events appear as linear bars for direct comparison.
>
> The tradeoffs remain explicit: tiny bubbles need focus, and grouped points need selection. Forty events with uncertain or incompatible quantities remain accessible separately.

Suggested timing: original purpose and critique, 0-15 seconds; two alternatives, 15-35 seconds; shared interactions, 35-50 seconds; limitations, 50-60 seconds.
