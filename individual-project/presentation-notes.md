# One-minute presentation

**Teresa Tu - STATS 401**

This is a 123-word speaking script, approximately one minute at a measured pace. Keep the single slide on screen throughout.

> I redesigned Information is Beautiful's data breach visualization. Its bubbles make major breaches memorable, but crowded circles and uneven labels make individual events difficult to compare.
>
> My D3 redesign starts with the ten largest reported counts. Every row shows the organization, year, and value. Horizontal bars share a zero baseline, so readers can compare lengths directly. Search and category filters narrow the question, and pagination keeps the remaining events accessible.
>
> I separate forty events whose counts are unknown, ambiguous, or incompatible with this numeric comparison. Their names and source explanations remain available.
>
> The tradeoff is that the first page does not show the whole dataset. Small counts need filtering or paging, and the reported quantities do not represent a count of unique people.

Suggested timing: original and critique, 0-15 seconds; redesign decisions, 15-40 seconds; data handling and tradeoff, 40-60 seconds.

Source quantities include different types of records and accounts, so the ranking compares reported quantities rather than verified counts of unique people affected.
