Design reflection

Design Justification

This page uses two complementary views of the same data. The clustered bubble view emphasizes regional structure. Cities are positioned inside one of four region panels, so region is encoded spatially. Bubble area represents population; a square-root radius scale keeps area proportional to population. Fill color moves from cool blue to warm red to represent average temperature, while outline thickness represents the ordered development levels Low, Medium, and High. The scatterplot provides a more precise comparison: population appears on the x-axis, temperature on the y-axis, region through color, and development level through point size. City labels and interactive tooltips identify every observation and reveal exact values. Using both views lets readers switch between seeing regional groups and examining quantitative relationships. The design makes large, warm, highly developed cities easy to notice while also helping viewers compare patterns across regions.
