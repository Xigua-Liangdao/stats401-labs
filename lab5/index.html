03 · Design description

Making attributes readable

Node-link design

Node color identifies district; symbol area scales linearly with daily passengers to emphasize busy stations, and shape distinguishes station types. Line width represents travel time; color and dash pattern identify service. Dragging separates overlaps, highlighting reveals neighbors, and tooltips supply exact values. The lower strip preserves isolated stations without implying geography.

Matrix design

Rows and columns represent stations. Filled cells show direct links, hue identifies route type, and opacity increases with travel time. District strips and ordering expose between-district patterns. Tooltips retain station attributes; passenger volume and station type are omitted from cell appearance to preserve readability.
